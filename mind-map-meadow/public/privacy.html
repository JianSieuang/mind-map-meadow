Mind Map Meadow is a small personal project: you log real tasks, habits and money, and a pixel town grows from it. This page says what it keeps about you, why, and how to take it or erase it.

What we keep

  • Your account: your name, email address and profile picture from the sign-in provider (Google today), the provider's account number, and your timezone.
  • What you enter: tasks and daily habits, money logs (amount, type, category, detail, date and any note you write), savings goals, and your keyboard shortcuts.
  • Your town: buildings, workers, coins and a record of every coin change, levels and progress.
  • Visit counts: for each day, which kinds of thing you did (opened the app, finished a task, logged money, collected, built, summoned) and how many times. Never what the task or log said.
  • Friends: who you sent or accepted friend requests to.
  • Companion notes: the short remarks the town's AI resident made.
  • One cookie that keeps you signed in. No advertising or tracking cookies, no analytics services.

Why

Only to run the game for you: show your town and lists, pay out rewards fairly, keep you signed in, and see whether people keep coming back so we know if the idea works. We don't sell your data, show ads, or share it with anyone for marketing.

The AI resident

The resident reads your town level, coins, building positions and the titles, categories and status of up to five tasks, so it can comment on them. Today it runs on a language model on our own server, so this stays on our machine.

Money logs and notes are never sent to any AI. If we move the resident to an outside AI service, this page will name it and say exactly what it receives before the switch.

Who can see what

  • You see everything above.
  • Friends you accept see your name, picture, email address and your town's buildings when they visit. Not your tasks, money or notes.
  • Anyone signed in can find you by part of your name or your exact email address when adding friends. They see your name and picture; your email stays hidden unless you accept them.
  • The person running the app can technically read the database, and uses it only to keep the app working and to count visits.

Where it's kept, and for how long

In a database on the app's server ([hosting provider: to fill in]). Everything is kept until you delete your account. Daily backups are kept for 14 days, so a deleted account is gone from backups within two weeks.

Your choices

  • Download your data: Profile → Download my data gives you a file with everything above.
  • Delete your account: Profile → Delete account erases it and everything it owns straight away, and signs you out everywhere.
  • Questions or requests: [contact email: to fill in].

Children

Mind Map Meadow isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly keep their data.

Changes

If this page changes in a way that matters, the app will tell you before it takes effect.
